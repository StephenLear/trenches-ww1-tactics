## What Didn't Work (and Fixes)

- **ArialHB.ttc font** → showed Unicode squares in FFmpeg drawtext → switched to `Verdana Bold.ttf`
- **drawbox coordinates using expressions** (e.g. `x=(w-640)/2`) → box appeared in top-left corner → fixed with explicit pixel values (x=210, y=1040)
- **Endcard missing from final video** → frame rate mismatch: gameplay clips at 60fps, endcard generated at 25fps → re-rendered endcard at 60fps with `-frames:v 180`, rebuilt concat
- **TikTok ad v1 (AI avatar)** → dropped at 0:01, 9% retention, "Can We Talk About" hook pattern-matched as ad → replaced with raw gameplay + text overlays

---

## Key Decisions

- **No AI avatar in TikTok ads**: WW1 history audience wants authenticity; avatars are immediately identified as ads and swiped
- **Lead with period quotes, not gameplay board**: The grid looks abstract at first glance; the gold quote banners carry the emotional weight
- **Subtitle uses battle names not genre**: "Somme to Verdun" resonates with UK audience (top territory) more than "Turn-Based Strategy Game"
- **Description + keywords deferred**: These fields are locked in App Store Connect until next binary submission — flagged for next build
- **Promotional text as priority fix**: Updates instantly with no review — filled a blank field that was wasting prime listing real estate

---

## Lessons Learned & Gotchas

- App Store subtitle/promotional text CAN be edited without a new binary; description and keywords CANNOT (locked to version record)
- Metadata-only App Store submissions go through quick review (hours, not days)
- TikTok's FYP distribution was fine (97.8%) — the problem was always content, not reach
- FFmpeg concat with `-c copy` silently drops clips with mismatched frame rates — always verify fps across all clips before concat
- The `zoompan` / `-loop 1` trap: always add `-frames:v N` on output side to cap duration
- GROWTH_PLAN.md is untracked in git — should be committed
